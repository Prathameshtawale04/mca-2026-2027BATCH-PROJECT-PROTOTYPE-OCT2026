Information Retrieval & Machine Learning Architecture

STEP 1: METADATA SOUP SYNTHESIS

Text Preprocessing & Feature Boosting

To capture both high-level semantic themes and stylistic nuances, each movie record is converted into a weighted metadata representation combining title, overview, boosted genres, boosted thematic keywords, director, and cast.

soup = [title, overview, genres*2, keywords*2, director*2, cast]
STEP 2: SUBLINEAR TF-IDF VECTORIZATION

Term Frequency & Inverse Document Frequency

Word frequencies undergo logarithmic sublinear scaling to prevent long synopses from artificially inflating scores. Rare, highly discriminatory tokens (e.g. director names, unique sci-fi terms) receive higher IDF penalty-weights.

$$\text{TF}(t, d) = 1 + \ln(\text{count}(t, d))$$ $$\text{IDF}(t) = \ln\left(\frac{1 + N}{1 + \text{DF}(t)}\right) + 1$$
STEP 3: COSINE SIMILARITY EVALUATION

Normalized Dot Product on Hypersphere

Because vectors are $L_2$-normalized onto the unit sphere, computing the cosine similarity simplifies to a high-speed sparse matrix inner product. Values range from $0.0$ (orthogonal) to $1.0$ (identical).

$$\cos(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 \|\mathbf{v}\|_2} = \sum_{k=1}^{V} u_k \cdot v_k$$
